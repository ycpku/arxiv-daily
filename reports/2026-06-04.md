  - Primary category: math.NA
  - Relevance score: 6
  - Abstract: The aim of this paper is to study the well-posedness and finite element approximation of the electrostatic shear Alfvén wave equations, a coupled system of two partial differential equations arising in plasma physics as a simplified sub-model of the drift-reduced Braginskii equations. To this end, anisotropic Sobolev spaces depending on the normalized magnetic field $\b$ are introduced, together with a Poincaré-type inequality along the integral curves of $\b$, which holds under a geometric directedness condition on the magnetic field. Using these tools, existence, uniqueness, and stability of a weak solution are established via the Faedo-Galerkin method.
